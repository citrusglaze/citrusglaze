# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** March 24, 2026

## What CitrusGlaze Does

CitrusGlaze is local security software that monitors AI tool traffic on your device. All processing and storage happens locally on your machine.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect your data.** CitrusGlaze processes data entirely on your local device. No data is transmitted to our servers.

- AI conversation content: stored locally on your device only
- Security scan results: stored locally on your device only
- No accounts, no cloud storage, no server-side processing

## Optional Telemetry

Telemetry is **disabled by default**. If you choose to enable it, anonymous product usage metadata (no conversation content) is sent to our analytics provider. You can disable it at any time in Settings.

## Browser Extension

The CitrusGlaze browser extension monitors AI chat websites to detect security risks. Captured data is sent to the locally-running CitrusGlaze application on your device (`127.0.0.1`). No data is sent to external servers.

## Third Parties

We do not sell, share, or transfer user data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising. We do not train AI models on your data.

## Data Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. Uninstall the software to delete all data, or use the built-in data management tools.

## Security

Data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption. Access is restricted to your user account via operating system permissions.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.
